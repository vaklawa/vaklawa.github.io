I Seek Solitude

I seek solitude; to be alone; to be calm.
I seek an exit; to swim away; to hide myself
From the cacophony of this confusing life,
This world, so full of people and noise
That makes me hurt so much,
I seek oblivion and contentment...

Nowhere on this earth, but on the island of my mind,

I seek a silent place; a ruin in the forest; a clearing,
Hearing the whispers of the fairies;
I seek a secret place; a fog-bound lake; a magic cave,
Sleeping in the velvet of the darkness.

I seek happiness and safety,
To give my pounding ears, my burning eyes, a break,
My brain, it swells in hyper-awareness; it hurts
I seek to escape...

Nowhere on this earth can relative calm become absolute, Nowhere on this earth, but on the island of my mind; Nowhere on this earth can true happiness be found, Nowhere on this earth, but on the island of my mind; Only on the island, only in my mind, Only in my mind, Only in my mind...

Only by myself can I be far from the pain;
Only by myself can I be free of all this dread,
In the oasis of confinement, where loneliness won't hurt;
The island of the mind, where I can be alone.

That is the end I seek.
