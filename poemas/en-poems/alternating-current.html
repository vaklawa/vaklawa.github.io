Alternating Current

This pain in my skull going ‘round like AC
Is the pain of obsession, of perfection,
Of not sleeping,

Of confining the force of Lightning
And the Earth’s magnetic field
To the size of a cigarette case

Or Christmas card, or a pill box
Or something even smaller like a cute pocket mirror
So you can take it anywhere.

This pain in my skull going ‘round like AC
Is the eternal throbbing headache
Of hunger and fascination,

The sacred exhaustion of a neverending job
Of shying away from the sun’s x-ray stare
Into the glaring white knives of the lab’s fluorescents

Where ten thousand living mice await
The experiments,
I can almost feel the weight of their sweet eyes as I pass by.

I am so utterly concentrated
There’s no way to break the spell;
Stopping now would cause
An electric arc inside me

They say if you put your fingers in a socket
AC’s whorls will trap you like a perfect spinning cobweb
Which is why you shouldn’t do that
Unless you want to die.

They say light and darkness can’t exist without one another
But my world has been shadowless for so long
I start to doubt it.

This pain in my skull going ‘round like AC
Never stops.

Man, I really need a short circuit.
